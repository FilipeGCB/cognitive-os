## Capability / companion status

No third-party companion is promoted as `supported` or installed by default in v1.4.0.

- `notebooklm-py` / NotebookLM MCP: candidate, Gauntlet status `test`; pinned at v0.8.2; specific consent and account authentication required. CI may prove package/entrypoint installability without proving user-account authentication or NotebookLM service behavior.
- OpenNotebookLM: preferred open-source candidate for the next direct grounded-corpus integration test; not default/supported.
- Open Notebook: candidate; not default/supported.
- SurfSense: `continue-research`; broader research platform and mixed licensing in part of the repository; not default/supported.
- AnythingLLM: candidate; not default/supported.
- Firecrawl: candidate for Structured Crawl; no bundled/default install.

Therefore the release does not claim runtime validation of a companion that has not been exercised with its required account/data context.
